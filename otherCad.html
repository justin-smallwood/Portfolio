Water propelled car

This project aimed to optimize the design of a water propelled car. This model consisted of a main car body that simultaneously serves as a water reservoir where an electric-powered pump delivered the water up a tube to be expelled at the water wheel, which then propelled the car in perpetual motion. In this team project, we measured the car’s horsepower output and compared it with actual car horsepower outputs to draw conclusions on the viability of water-propelled cars. I was responsible for entirely designing and 3D printing the parts of the car.
